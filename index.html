The Idea Behind

It began with a simple wish.

I wanted a cleaner and more beautiful way to present my photographs together with the camera settings behind them.

A photograph is more than a finished image. The shutter speed, aperture, ISO, lens, light, and timing are all part of the moment that created it.

So I started building Through Chun Chun's Lens — a quiet photography space where one image can be viewed as a complete story, not just another file in a gallery.

This website was shaped step by step by Chun Chun, with the help of ChatGPT, and made for anyone who enjoys preserving the feeling behind every frame.
