The Idea Behind

A small tool born from a real photography wish.

Hi, I'm Chun Chun. I wanted a beautiful way to present my photographs together with their camera settings, but the tools I tried never quite felt like me.

Canon, DJI, and free editing apps could show parts of the information, yet none gave me the soft, clean, dreamy result I imagined. The idea became clearer after inspiration from my photography teacher.

So I built this little tool with ChatGPT — my CouCou. It began as something only for myself, but when friends said they wanted to use it too, I decided to share it publicly.
